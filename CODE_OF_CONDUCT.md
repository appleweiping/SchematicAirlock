# Community Conduct

This project welcomes careful technical disagreement and people with varied
experience. Participate with patience, state evidence clearly, and focus
critique on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, repeated
disruption, or pressuring someone to disclose confidential designs or models.
Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these standards
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's published
GitHub contact channel. Include links and relevant context. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical; conflicts of interest should be
disclosed so another maintainer can respond.
